# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and maintainers pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

Cybersecurity communities have a well-known problem with gatekeeping and hostility toward beginners. This project explicitly rejects that culture. Asking a "basic" question is always welcome here.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language.
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experience levels.
- Gracefully accepting constructive criticism.
- Focusing on what is best for the community and for learners.
- Showing empathy toward other community members.

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments, public or private.
- "Gatekeeping" — belittling newcomers for their skill level.
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission.
- Sharing or encouraging unauthorized access to real-world systems.
- Any conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions) and in public spaces when an individual is representing the project.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to the project maintainers. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated and will result in a response deemed necessary and appropriate to the circumstances. Maintainers are obligated to maintain confidentiality with regard to the reporter of an incident.
