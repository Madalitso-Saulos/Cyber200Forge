# Objectives

After completing this challenge, you should be able to:

1. Explain what Least Significant Bit (LSB) steganography is and why it's difficult to detect visually.
2. Use `zsteg` to automatically scan a PNG for common LSB patterns.
3. Use `steghide` to extract data hidden with a passphrase.
4. Recognize that steganography detection often requires trial and error across multiple tools, since the extraction method must match the embedding method.
